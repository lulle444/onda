Our commitments

A token never touches the ranking

01

Rankings stay independent

A token changes nothing about which pools are listed, how they’re sorted or how they’re scored. Every pool goes through the same public risk method, and no protocol can buy its way up, with tokens or anything else.

02

No promises

We don’t promise returns, buybacks, airdrops or features that don’t exist yet. A token isn’t a share in Basewatch and gives no claim on revenue, votes or data.

03

Holdings in the open

Every team wallet that holds the token, and how much it holds, is listed on this page. If the team ever sells, we’ll say so on X before or as it happens.

Stay safe

How to spot a fake

The only official contract address is the one on this page, also pinned on @usebasewatch. Tokens with our name on other chains, other addresses or in DMs are not ours.

We will never ask you to connect a wallet, send funds or “claim” anything. Basewatch itself stays read-only.
