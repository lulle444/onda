Token

The Tripod token.

What’s official, how to check it, and the rules that keep Tripod’s basket levels independent of any token.

01 · Independent

Levels stay untouched

A token changes nothing about which baskets are listed, which tokens appear or how any level is calculated. Every basket uses the same public method.

02 · No promises

No returns promised

No promised returns, buybacks, airdrops or features that don’t exist yet. A token isn’t a share in Tripod and gives no claim on revenue, votes or data.

03 · In the open

Holdings disclosed

Every team wallet holding the token, and how much it holds, is shared on X. If the team ever sells, we’ll say so on X before or as it happens.

Stay safe

How to spot a fake

The only official address is the one pinned on @usetripod. Tokens with our name at other addresses, on other chains or offered in DMs are not ours.

We never ask you to connect a wallet

Tripod stays read-only. We will never ask you to connect a wallet, send funds or “claim” anything.

Read this before you buy anything

Tokens like this are highly speculative. Most lose most of their value, liquidity can be thin, and you can lose everything you put in. Rules for crypto-assets differ by country, including the EU’s MiCA rules. Nothing here is financial advice or an offer to buy or sell.
